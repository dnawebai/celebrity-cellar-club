# OPUS DRINKS

## Members Exclusive

Welcome to the most exclusive wine and beverage membership experience in North America. Since 2023 and with over 24,000 active members.

The OPUS Drinks is a private, invitation-based membership program created for wine collectors, beverage enthusiasts, luxury consumers, and individuals seeking access to rare and highly sought-after wines, spirits, and celebrity-endorsed beverages.

### Membership Application

Access to the Opus Club is subject to approval.

Prospective members must complete the Membership Application Form and submit all requested information for review.

A one-time, non-refundable Application Fee of $99 USD is required at the time of submission.

All applications are reviewed individually by the Opus Club Membership Committee.

Completed applications shall be automatically submitted to:

[elena@grusdrinks.com](mailto:elena@grusdrinks.com)

Applicants will be notified by email regarding approval status.

### Membership Benefits

Upon approval, members will be enrolled in the Opus Club Membership Program.

Membership Fee:
$199 USD per month or  yearly (10% discount)

Membership Includes:

• Four premium bottles delivered directly to the member's home every month.
• Access to limited-production wines and spirits.
• Access to member-only auctions.
• Invitations to exclusive tasting events.
• Early access to new releases.
• Access to the Beverages by Influencers marketplace.
• Priority access to celebrity beverage collections.
• Exclusive member discounts.

Currently available only to residents of the United States.

# OPUS BY INFLUENCERS

## Exclusive Member Marketplace

Opus by Influencers is a private marketplace available exclusively to approved Opus Club members.

Each month, Opus Drinks partners with globally recognized celebrities, influencers, athletes, entertainers, and entrepreneurs to feature beverages they personally enjoy and recommend.

Members gain access to exclusive monthly auctions and limited-edition allocations.

### Monthly Celebrity Auctions

Every month, a collection of 100 bottles selected by the featured influencer will be made available through a members-only auction.

Examples of Featured Influencers:

• Nicole Kidman
• Selena Gomez
• Miley Cyrus
• Dwayne Johnson
• Additional celebrities and influencers announced monthly

### Marketplace Experience

The portal will display each featured influencer as an interactive profile image.

clicking on an image will have access to:

• Influencer portrait
• Biography
• Favorite wines and spirits for sale (will be added by backoffice of the platform and will be for sale at $200 bottle. members can by and will be shipped to their address in US.
• Current auction items
• Buy Now opportunities
• Auction participation

Members may browse each influencer's collection and purchase available products directly through the platform.

The Opus Club offers sponsors direct exposure to a highly engaged audience of wine collectors, luxury consumers, investors, executives, and beverage enthusiasts.

Sponsors may purchase advertising placements throughout:

• Opus Club Portal
• Opus by Influencers

• Auction Pages
